Regulatory Compliance

Project develops innovative solutions that enable you to solve your formulation difficulties. As regulatory compliance is one of our essential commitments, Project's Regulatory Affairs team closely monitors the continuously increasing regulatory requirements around the world.

Project promotes the safe use of its products to protect people and the environment. The Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS's) are the primary vehicle for transmitting health, safety, environmental and disposal information on our products.

We expect our customers to understand the nature of the hazards associated with the products, and thus to use the product safely and in compliance with relevant regulations. At Project we are committed to making regulatory compliance easier for you.

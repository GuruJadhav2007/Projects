Who We Are

Powering Industries
Through Chemistry

Project Pvt. Ltd. is an organization committed to providing research & development, manufacturing and product support for a spectrum of industries related to oil and natural gas sector, water treatment, specialty chemicals, polymers and pharmaceuticals.

At Project, we believe in the Power of Chemistry. We believe in creating enduring products developed through high quality innovation and efficient manufacturing processes with utmost concern towards the Environment, Health & Safety.

Our manpower consists of professionals with many years of experience in research and development and manufacturing of various chemical entities.

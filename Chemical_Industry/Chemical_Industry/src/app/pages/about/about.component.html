About Project

Our Story

Powering Industry Through the Power of Chemistry

Project Pvt. Ltd. is an organization committed to provide research and development, manufacturing and product support for a spectrum of industries related to oil and natural gas sector, water treatment sector, specialty chemicals and polymers and pharmaceuticals.

At Project, we believe in the Power of Chemistry. We believe in creating enduring products developed through high quality of innovation and efficient manufacturing processes with utmost concern towards the Environment, Health & Safety.

It is our constant endeavor to live up to this expectation as this is the key to Sustainable Success. Our manpower consists of professionals with many years of experience in the field of research and development and manufacturing of various chemical entities.

As a contract research and manufacturing organization, our portfolio is large to cater requirements of various petroleum, chemical and pharmaceutical companies to develop their product range by offering committed intermediates and support.
